How to play

  1. Pick up to 5 beings and Cast. Matching Auras (the coloured gem on each card) make the hand.
  2. Hit the target in 4 casts to break through. Not happy with your hand? Discard up to 5 cards and draw new ones (3 times a layer).
  3. Break through and choose a new being for your deck. Eight layers to the Source, then keep going as deep as you dare.
