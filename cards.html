REALM

The Card Game

Your beings are your champions. One move a turn. Knock out all three to win.

How to play

  1. You and the rival each have three champions. Each has HP and Attack; rarer beings have more of both.
  2. On your turn, do one thing: tap one of your champions then an enemy to attack, or play a spell.
  3. Knock out all three rival champions to win.
