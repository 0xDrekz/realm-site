REALM

The Card Game

Your beings are your champions. The First Rites are your spells. Knock out all three of the rival's champions to win.

How to play

  1. Champions. You field three beings. Their HP and attacks come from their five stats; rarer beings last longer and hit harder.
  2. Essence. You get one more every turn (up to 8). Spend it on ritual cards: damage, heals, shields and tricks.
  3. Attack once a turn. Tap a champion, pick a stat, pick a target. You hit for a quarter of that stat.
  4. Elements. Light beats Dark, Dark beats Spirit, Spirit beats Magic, Magic beats Knowledge, Knowledge beats Light: 1.5x damage. A champion's element is its highest stat.
