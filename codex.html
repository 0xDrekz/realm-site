The Card Codex

1,111 beings. 1,111 cards.

Every REALM being is a card in the coming battler. The same being always makes the same card: its five essences set its power, and its traits give it its powers. Find the one you hold, or the one you'll mint.

Reading the cards…

Reading a card

  • Cost (top left): the essence it takes to summon. It comes from the tier: Common 1, Uncommon and Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4, Mythic 5, Entity 6, God 7, the Source 8.
  • Power and health (bottom): what it hits for, and what it takes to bring it down.
  • Essence: its strongest stat, sometimes two. The colour of the card.
  • Arrive: happens when it is summoned.
  • Flying: only flying units can block it. Haste: attacks the turn it arrives. Veiled: its first attack can't be blocked. Unblockable: never blocked.
  • Lifelink: the damage it deals heals you. Poison: destroys any unit it damages. Freeze: a unit it damages can't attack next turn. First strike: hits before the other side. Double strike: hits first, then again.
  • Six one-of traits give a unique power: Tempest, Grove, Forest, Heavy Dust, Barrage and Cluster. Gods, Entities and the Source have their own.
