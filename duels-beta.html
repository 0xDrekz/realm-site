      How to play

      1. Win by bringing the rival's life (the heart) to 0.
      2. Each turn you get essence (gold diamonds): one more than last turn. Cards cost essence.
      3. Play cards from your hand. Gold cards are your champions, your own beings. Units go on your side of the table. Rituals are one-off spells.
      4. New units can't attack straight away. Next turn they show READY: tap Attack and they hit the rival.
      5. When the rival attacks, your units can block. We pick sensible blocks for you; tap a unit to change it.
      6. Tap any card or unit to read it.

      Choose your team

      Up to three of your beings. Rarer beings are stronger, and your $DMT boost makes all of them hit harder.
