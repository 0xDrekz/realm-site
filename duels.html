A game of the realm

REALM Duels

Three cards. Three rounds. Rarer rivals every stage. How deep can your beings go?

How it works

  1. Every stage you are dealt three cards: three of your own beings at random, or borrowed spirits if you have none. Each card has five powers: Magic, Spirit, Knowledge, Light and Dark, shaped by its traits. Rarer beings have more.
  2. Each round you both play a card face down. The caller names a stat and the higher number takes the round. You call rounds 1 and 3, the rival calls round 2.
  3. Win two rounds to clear the stage. Every stage the rivals get rarer. One loss ends the run.

Your best run of the week goes on the board. Prizes in $DMT for the top of the board are announced in the Telegram.

Play with your beings

Nothing is connected or signed. The board lists the wallet that played.
