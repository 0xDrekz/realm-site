How it works

  1. Every round you are dealt a fresh hand of three: your own beings at random, or borrowed spirits if you have none. You play one. Each card has five powers: Magic, Spirit, Knowledge, Light and Dark, shaped by its traits. Rarer beings have more.
  2. You and the rival each play a card face down. The caller names a stat and the higher number takes the round. You call rounds 1 and 3, the rival calls round 2.
  3. Win two rounds to clear the stage. Every stage the rivals get rarer. One loss ends the run.

Your best run of the week goes on the board. Prizes in $DMT for the top of the board are announced in the Telegram.

The $DMT boost
