How it is worked out

Your points = the weights of your beings added up × your multiplier.

Your payout = the pool × your points ÷ everyone's points added up.

  • Reward — what your beings receive. Rewards are paid once, when all are minted, and by then the pool and the total weight are both fixed: every weight point is worth the pool ÷ .
  • boost — holding multiplies your weight, so you take a bigger slice. How much bigger depends on everyone else: the figure shown is the most it could add, if nobody else held any. If everyone holds the same band, the multipliers cancel and you receive your reward. If others hold more than you, some of your reward goes to them.
