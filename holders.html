How it is worked out

Your points = the weights of your beings added up × your multiplier.

Your payout = the pool × your points ÷ everyone's points added up.

Because everyone else's counts against yours, nobody can know their exact payout before the snapshot. That is why this page always gives a range:

  • Low — every other holder is in the top band.
  • Typical — everyone is in the same band as you. The multipliers cancel out, so this is your weight's share of all the weight.
  • High — nobody else holds any .

All three assume every being is minted. A smaller mint means a smaller pool, shared among fewer beings. on its own earns nothing; it only multiplies beings. This is an estimate, not a promise, and not financial advice.
