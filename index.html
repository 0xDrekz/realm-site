FAQ

How many are there in total?

, and that is all there will ever be.

Is this a staged release?

No. Every being mints at once, at one price. There are no rounds, no phases and no waves — when it is open it is open, and when it closes it is finished.

How many can I mint?

per wallet.

What does it cost?

each, the same for every being whatever tier it turns out to be. There is no early price and no late price.

What chain is REALM on?

Solana. You'll need a Solana wallet such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, and some SOL for the mint and network fees.

When do I find out what I got?

Immediately. There is no delayed reveal — you see your being the moment it is minted. You cannot choose your tier, but you are not kept waiting to learn it either, and you can decide whether to mint again knowing what you already hold.

How do I know it isn't rigged?

The whole collection is made before the mint opens, and a provenance hash of it is published before anyone can buy. Every image is hashed, the hashes are joined in token order, and that is hashed again. Afterwards anyone can repeat it on the finished collection: if a single being had been altered, or two swapped around, the hash would not match.

Be clear about what that does and does not prove. It proves the collection handed out is the collection that was hashed. It does not by itself prove how mint order was assigned to token number — that is the launchpad’s shuffle, and its fairness rests on the launchpad, not on us.

What if it doesn't sell out?

There is no closing date. The gate stays open until all are minted, and the holder pool is paid once, when the last one goes. Until then the pool grows with every mint, and nothing is paid out early.

That means the payout waits on the sell-out. If the realm fills slowly, holders wait longer; the share each being carries does not change.

Are the common ones worthless?

No, but be clear about what they are. A Common carries one weight point, and at the even field it returns less than it cost to mint — the rare tiers are paid for by the common ones. What a Common does carry is the same art quality as everything else in the collection — one grid, one pipeline, no cheap tier.

What is the Source?

The 1,111th being: the Prime Source, a star of gold light with one eye at its heart. There is exactly one, it carries 111 weight points, and there will never be another.

Is there a royalty on resales?

on every secondary sale. It is separate from the holder pool, which is 75% of the mint and is paid once, when the last being is minted.

When does minting open?

When the gate is opened. Until then the mint control on this site says so plainly rather than taking anything.

Will there be another collection?

These 1,111 are closed once they are minted and nothing will ever be added to them. Whether anything else is ever made is a separate question with a separate answer, and no promise is being made here either way.
