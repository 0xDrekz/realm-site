FAQ

What is the $DMT contract address?

Only this one, on Solana: Always check it here before you buy anywhere.

What is REALM?

$DMT, the REALM token, and BREAKTHROUGH, a free card game you play on your phone or in Telegram. Hold $DMT and every cast you make scores more.

Does it cost anything to play?

No. BREAKTHROUGH is free, with no entry fee and nothing at stake. You can play as a guest, or sign in with a Solana wallet to use your holder boost and enter the competitions.

How does the holder boost work?

Sign in with the wallet that holds your $DMT. The game reads its live balance and adds the boost to every cast, up to × at the top band. See the bands under $DMT.

How do the competitions work?

Your best run on the Daily Trip, the deck everyone plays that day, goes on the board. Entry is free: sign in with a wallet and play as many times as you like. Prizes come only from the team’s own funds. Every run is replayed on the server, so a score can’t be faked.

Are there more games coming?

Yes. New games will use the same cards and the same holder boost. BREAKTHROUGH comes first.

What chain is it on?

Solana. To use the boost you need a Solana wallet such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack.
