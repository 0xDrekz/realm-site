    FAQ

    How many are there in total?

    , and that is all there will ever be. Ten sectors of 111, and one more — the Source — counted in the tenth.

    Is this a staged release?

    No. Every being mints at once, at one price. The ten sectors are where a being comes from and how the story is told; they are not a schedule.

    How many can I mint?

    per wallet.

    What does it cost?

    each, the same for every being whatever tier it turns out to be. There is no early price and no late price.

    What chain is REALM on?

    Solana. You'll need a Solana wallet such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, and some SOL for the mint and network fees.

    Do I know which tier I'm getting?

    No. Every being stays sealed until the mint closes and the reveal happens. That keeps the rare tiers fair.

    What if it doesn't sell out?

    The gate shuts on whether or not every being is taken, and the pool pays on what was actually minted. The pool is a share of the money that came in, not a fixed sum, so a smaller mint means a smaller pool and nothing is owed that does not exist. Nobody is left waiting for a sell-out that may not come.

    Are the common ones worthless?

    No, but be clear about what they are. A Common carries one weight point, and at the even field it returns less than it cost to mint — the rare tiers are paid for by the common ones. What a Common does carry is its sector's story, and the same art quality as everything else in the collection.

    What is the Source?

    The 1,111th being. There is exactly one, it carries 111 weight points, and there will never be another. It is the only being in the collection that is not one of ten of its kind.

    Is there a royalty on resales?

    on every secondary sale. It is separate from the holder pool, which is 75% of the mint and is paid once when the mint closes.

    When does minting open?

    When the gate is opened. Until then the mint control on this site says so plainly rather than taking anything.

    Will there be another collection?

    These 1,111 are closed once they are minted and nothing will ever be added to them. Whether anything else is ever made is a separate question with a separate answer, and no promise is being made here either way.
