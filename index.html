The Rounds

Ten rounds, one sector each. A round opens, its beings are taken, and the realm reaches one sector further in. The tenth holds 112 rather than 111, which is what makes 1,111. After it there is no eleventh.

    FAQ

    How many are there in total?

    1,111. Nine rounds of 111 and a tenth of 112 — the final sector holds one more than the rest, and that is the last being there will ever be.

    How many can I mint?

    Three per wallet, per round.

    What chain is REALM on?

    Solana. You'll need a Solana wallet such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, and some SOL for the mint and network fees.

    Do I know which tier I'm getting?

    No. Every being stays sealed until the round finishes and the reveal happens. That keeps the rare tiers fair.

    Are the common ones worthless?

    No. Commons and Uncommons can be chosen as Witnesses each round, and they carry the story of their sector.

    What happens after round 10?

    The realm is complete, the Source opens, and the collection closes forever. There is no round 11.

    What does it cost?

    Round one is . Every round after costs 0.1 SOL more than the one before, so the tenth and last is 1.05 SOL. The earlier you arrive, the less the realm asks of you.

    Is there a royalty on resales?

    on every secondary sale. That is what funds what is shared with holders — if nobody trades, there is nothing to share.

    When does minting open?

    When the gate for the round is opened. Until then the mint control on this site says so plainly rather than taking anything.
