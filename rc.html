How to play

  1. Bring your rival from 20 life to 0.
  2. Crystals pay for cards. You get one more every turn, up to 10, and they refill each turn.
  3. Beings fight for you. They can't attack the turn they arrive (unless they have Haste).
  4. Attack once a turn with any of your ready beings. Your rival chooses blockers: one blocker per attacker. Unblocked attackers hit the rival.
  5. Blocked beings trade damage: power against health. Damage heals at the end of each turn.
  6. Rites are fast: you can cast them while your rival is attacking you. Rituals are cast on your own turn. Relics stay in play.
  7. Essences are like colours: Magic burns, Spirit grows, Knowledge draws and freezes, Light flies and protects, Dark destroys. A deck uses one or two.
  8. Tap a card to play it. Hold any card to read it.
  9. Win to earn stars and climb from Bronze to Mythic. The higher you go, the smarter your rivals.
